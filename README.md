Privacy Policy for OnVantage

Last updated: May 2026

OnVantage does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

DATA STORAGE
All data you create in the app — categories, challenges, streaks, and progress —
is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

ICLOUD SYNC
If you are signed into iCloud on your device, your data may be synced across
your personal Apple devices via Apple's CloudKit service. This sync happens
privately within your own iCloud account. OnVantage has no access to this data
and it is never shared with third parties. You can disable iCloud sync at any
time in your device's Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud.

NOTIFICATIONS
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are never sent through
external servers.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party
services are used.
